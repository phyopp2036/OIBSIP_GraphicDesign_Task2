Task 2: Logo Design - Golden Five

Objective
To design a luxury brand logo and visual identity for a premium tile and home interior brand.

Tools Used Canva 

Steps Performed

Background & Theme: Selected a dark, luxury-themed background with brilliant golden glitter and starburst lighting effects to create a premium atmosphere.

Central Frame: Added a clean, metallic golden square frame in the center to focus the viewer's attention directly on the brand identity.

Logo Development: Crafted a minimalist geometric house logo using sharp golden lines to symbolize architecture, premium tiles, and home interiors.

Brand Text Styling: Positioned the brand title "GOLDEN FIVE" in a bold, clean sans-serif font using a vibrant gold color to match the theme.

Tagline Alignment: Placed the core company motto "PERFECT TILES. PERFECT HOMES." centered at the bottom using crisp black lettering for strong contrast and excellent readability.
